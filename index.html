About me

I am an associate professor at AMSS-CAS in Beijing, China since July 2021. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher working with Prof. Victor Magron and Prof. Jean-Bernard Lasserre at LAAS-CNRS in Toulouse, France, from July 2019 to June 2021.

Before that, I was a postdoc working with Prof. Bican Xia at Peking University. I received my doctoral degree in mathematics under the supervision of Prof. Xiaoshan Gao at AMSS-CAS in July 2017.
